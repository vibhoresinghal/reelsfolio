About these metrics

Visitors are recognized browsers, not verified people. Visits include repeat visits and additional tabs. The chart shows unique visitors per day; rolling date ranges can include partial first and last days. Comparisons use the previous period; all-time totals have no comparison.

Watch time is reported playback time and may include buffering or repeat loops. It is not a completion rate. Reports include up to eight projects per metric, so watch-time totals reflect that set.

Session duration runs from the first to the last recorded event. Page load is the browser's load event, not time until a video becomes playable. Missing measurements appear as a dash.

About these metrics

Views are recorded video-view events, not unique people. Watch / view divides recorded watch time by view events. Percentages and bars show each project's share of reported watch time. Reports include up to eight entries per metric; a dash means that measurement isn't available.
