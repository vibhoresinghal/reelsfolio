# Tree-shadow assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-08. Five independent original PNG shadow assets, used as alpha masks. The supplied warm-wall/tree-shadow references informed the art direction; no pixels were extracted from the watermarked stock preview. Source outputs are preserved in the Codex generated-images folder.

The controller offers 45 **looks**, composed from five tree assets × nine lighting/projection treatments. It does not claim 45 independent tree images.

## Final prompts

### afternoon-branch.png

Create a NEW original production-ready photographic TREE SHADOW OVERLAY asset for a website environment, not a scene photograph. Single square 1024x1024 PNG with genuine alpha transparency. Only neutral near-black cast shadows of a real deciduous tree's connected branches, twigs and overlapping leaves; all gaps and background fully transparent. Shadows have physically natural varying opacity and penumbra, slightly out-of-focus edges, some sharper twigs, airy irregular holes. Aim for the feeling of warm late-afternoon sunlight projecting a real tree onto a wall, but DO NOT render the wall, window, lighting rectangle, textures or any background. This is the isolated shadow only. No checkerboard, text, watermarks, borders, colored leaves, vector illustration, radial blobs, repeated icons, geometric ellipses or decorative pattern. Organic botanical branching is important: leaves visibly grow along connected tapering twigs, with natural irregular lengths, overlaps and asymmetry. Composition is edge-anchored so it can crop naturally into a window, with a lot of clear negative space toward the opposite corner. Shadow silhouette should occupy about 55 percent of the square, on transparent background. Treatment 1 — AFTERNOON BRANCH: a substantial diagonal tree limb enters from the lower-left edge, forks into smaller limbs toward upper center and upper-right, with loose clusters of medium oval deciduous leaves. Calm organic branch structure, alternating leaf orientations, dappled sun gaps. Most shadow mass toward left and upper side, lower-right open. Mid-soft shadows, convincing depth variation, evocative of a tree's shadow on an old plaster wall.
