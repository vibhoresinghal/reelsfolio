# Production olive foliage asset

`olive-bough.png` is the only foliage texture used by the site. It was generated
with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-08 and is used as an alpha mask.
Reference images informed the art direction; no pixels were extracted from a
watermarked stock preview. The generation prompt is retained below.

### olive-bough.png

Create a NEW original production-ready photographic TREE SHADOW OVERLAY asset for a website environment, not a scene photograph. Single square 1024x1024 PNG with genuine alpha transparency. Only neutral near-black cast shadows of a real deciduous tree's connected branches, twigs and overlapping leaves; all gaps and background fully transparent. Shadows have physically natural varying opacity and penumbra, slightly out-of-focus edges, some sharper twigs, airy irregular holes. Aim for the feeling of warm late-afternoon sunlight projecting a real tree onto a wall, but DO NOT render the wall, window, lighting rectangle, textures or any background. This is the isolated shadow only. No checkerboard, text, watermarks, borders, colored leaves, vector illustration, radial blobs, repeated icons, geometric ellipses or decorative pattern. Organic botanical branching is important: leaves visibly grow along connected tapering twigs, with natural irregular lengths, overlaps and asymmetry. Composition is edge-anchored so it can crop naturally into a window, with a lot of clear negative space toward the opposite corner. Shadow silhouette should occupy about 55 percent of the square, on transparent background. Treatment 3 — OLIVE BOUGH: an old olive tree branch enters from the left edge and forks toward upper-right and lower center. Naturally arranged pairs of slender lance-shaped olive leaves on branching, gently kinked woody stems. Varied foreshortening, overlap, irregular clustering, an open Mediterranean sun-dappled feel. Left portion substantial, middle sparse, right edge clear. Moderately soft penumbra with some leaves slightly out of focus.
